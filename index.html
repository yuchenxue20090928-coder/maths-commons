How this works

  1. Write the question the way it actually confused you. A photo of your working beats a tidy rewrite.
  2. It arrives in my inbox with the paper and the date attached.
  3. If it is worth it, it gets a number and goes into the problem index — written solution first, recording if it earns one.

Nothing is published with your name on it unless you ask.
